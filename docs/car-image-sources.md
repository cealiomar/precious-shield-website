## Integrity

Lossless WebP conversion. Visible pixels were compared against downloaded PNG originals on both light and dark backgrounds and are identical. Alpha channels match exactly, preserving source shadows. No recoloring, background generation, retouching, stretching, mirroring or vehicle filters.

The same source is reused across product/hero/feature views where appropriate. Two independent Mercedes photos are displayed whole in the finish gallery, with compare/full-gloss/full-satin controls. Different colors, cameras and lighting are explicitly described; the images are not spliced or called before/after.
